Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. When it happens in Salem, Salem Restoration Co is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Warning Signs for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle
  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it
  • Do not wait on a slow drain, which can turn into a full blockage
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Food waste, oils and grease block pipes, so put them in the trash and not the sink
  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
