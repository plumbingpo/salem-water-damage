Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. Salem Restoration Co provides emergency water extraction throughout Salem using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Our truck-mounted extraction units are among the most powerful water removal systems available, with the suction to pull water from carpets, hard floors and structural materials far more effectively than consumer or rental equipment. Confined spaces, wall cavities and the gaps between floor layers call for different gear, so we use portable extractors, specialty tools and submersible pumps in places the truck-mounted hoses cannot get to.

Professional water extraction is not simply about removing the standing water you can see. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season so you know it will run
  • Program (530) 348-7175 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency
  • Look beneath sinks, behind toilets and beside appliances every so often to catch leaks early
  • Worn or aging hoses on washers, dishwashers and ice makers should be replaced before they fail
  • Leak-sensing shutoff valves can cut off the water on their own when something fails
  • Critical areas of the house should each have a leak sensor that you know is working

Signs It Is Time for Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water accumulating after a fire suppression system discharges
  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance
  • Water entering through a flooded basement stairwell or window well
  • Ceilings, light fixtures or walls that are actively leaking water
  • Storm water collecting in the lowest level of the home
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • Pooled water on the carpet, or a soggy feel with every step

Why Hire a Professional for Water Extraction?

How fast and how completely water is extracted decides how much of your property is damaged and what restoration will cost. A shop vacuum might need a whole day for what our truck-mounted equipment finishes in minutes, because professional extraction outpaces consumer gear hundreds of times over. This speed difference is not just about convenience, it is about preventing thousands of dollars in additional damage that occurs with every hour water stays in contact with building materials.

Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too. Through capillary action and wicking, water moves up drywall, into the pad under carpet, between layers of flooring and along plumbing lines and electrical conduit. We go after those hidden deposits with specialty tools and methods, and that takes out far more water than extracting the surface only. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.
