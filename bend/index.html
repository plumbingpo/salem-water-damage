Runoff: Rain running into the house at ground level ranks a close second. You cannot stop the rain, but you can move belongings up and keep away from any outlets near the water. Kitchens: Leaks from a dishwasher, an ice maker line or the plumbing under the kitchen sink should be dealt with the day you find them. Odors: A musty smell near the site of an old leak is the least urgent item here, yet it deserves an inspection soon.
