Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Shut off the water at the main whenever you leave home for more than a few days.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.
  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
  • In basements and other low areas, keep documents and valuables up on shelves.
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season and consider a battery backup.

How to Tell You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • The house was flooded when you walked in, with no telling how many hours it had been going.
  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.
  • A growing pool on the floor is heading for the next room or running downstairs.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • Several hours or more have passed with hardwood, drywall or carpet sitting wet.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • Floor-level power strips, plugged-in appliances or the breaker panel are now wet.
